The optional OpenMP physical kernel uses LLVM OpenMP from Emscripten 6.0.3 (Apache-2.0 WITH LLVM-exception; licenses/OpenMP.txt). Its pthread-enabled Fortran runtime is rebuilt from r-wasm/llvm-project commit 7ca73ca1ab129c86e63fd3a25aaa58bbf4b5d88c. ROMS module THREADPRIVATE variables use a limited native-WASM-TLS compatibility adapter. Numerical equations are unchanged; ROMS memory.F has matching VMEMORY critical-region names. See docs/CPU_PARALLEL_TRIAL.md for validation scope and limitations.

Land masks in assets/coastlines/n03-2026/ are processed from MLIT National Land Numerical Information (Administrative Zones, N03, 2026-01-01), Hokkaido and regional datasets 52-59. License: CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Source: https://nlftp.mlit.go.jp/ksj/gml/datalist/KsjTmplt-N03-2026.html . Municipal polygons are spatially indexed, stripped of non-geometric attributes and rounded to 1e-7 degrees; they are unioned during classification and for display-only exterior coastlines. No MLIT endorsement is implied. Source and derived hashes, CRS handling and limitations are retained with the data. These data are not MIT-licensed software.

Spatial initial fields in assets/climatology/woa23/ are processed from NOAA NCEI World Ocean Atlas 2023 objectively analyzed climatology (https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/world-ocean-atlas). Temperature/salinity use the 2015-2022 B5C2 quarter-degree fields, nutrients use 1965-2022 all-data one-degree fields. These are observations-based climatologies, not dynamical reanalyses. Original grid profiles within Japan and surrounding seas are retained; local horizontal and vertical interpolation, annual substitution for seasonal gaps, and vertical endpoint approximation are application processing. Original/derived checksums and URLs are retained with the atlas. Acknowledge NOAA NCEI when using or redistributing the derived data.

Terrain contour export uses d3-contour 4.0.2 (ISC), with d3-array and internmap (ISC), bundled as vendor/contours.js. License texts are retained under licenses/. Contour lines are derived from the edited model grid, not original survey contours.

Excel condition reports use write-excel-file 2.3.10 (MIT), bundled as vendor/write-excel-file.js. Its license is licenses/write-excel-file.txt. Bundled dependencies include JSZip (MIT), file-saver (MIT), pako (MIT/Zlib), lie, immediate, setimmediate and readable-stream with its supporting modules; their notices are retained under licenses/.

Surface chlorophyll in src/data/chlorophyll.js is NASA/GSFC OBPG MODIS Aqua cumulative mean (January 2003 through February 2019), distributed by NOAA OceanWatch. The exact subset URL, checksum, spatial reduction and valid-cell counts are recorded in that file. Source: https://oceanwatch.pifsc.noaa.gov/erddap/griddap/aqua_chla_climatology_2018_0.html . Carbon:chlorophyll, Redfield conversion, vertical profiles and inferred Z/D concentrations are modelling assumptions, not remotely sensed observations.

Wind samples in src/data/wind-ncep.js are derived from NCEP/NCAR Reanalysis 1 monthly 10 m winds provided by NOAA PSL (https://psl.noaa.gov/data/gridded/data.ncep.reanalysis.html). These are not ERA5 data or native reanalysis surface-stress products.
